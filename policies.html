Privacy Policy

Order process karne ke liye hum naam, mobile number, delivery address, order details aur payment reference jaisi zaroori information le sakte hain. Is information ka use order fulfilment, customer support aur service communication ke liye hota hai.

Hum customer information advertisers ko sell nahi karte. Information sirf order delivery, payment verification, legal compliance ya service providers ke saath utni hi share ki ja sakti hai jitni service poori karne ke liye zaroori ho.

Payment Policy

Website par available options me UPI aur Cash on Delivery shamil ho sakte hain. UPI payment karte waqt customer ko amount aur recipient details verify karni chahiye. Payment confirmation ke liye transaction reference/UTR manga ja sakta hai.

COD availability delivery location aur order value ke hisaab se confirm ki ja sakti hai. Suspicious, incomplete ya unverifiable orders ko hold ya cancel kiya ja sakta hai.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Order confirmation ke baad dispatch aur estimated delivery information customer ko order page, WhatsApp ya phone ke madhyam se di jayegi. Delivery time destination, courier availability, holidays aur weather jaise factors par depend kar sakta hai.

Customer ko sahi naam, phone number, PIN code aur complete address dena zaroori hai. Incorrect address ya customer unavailable hone se hone wali delay ke liye DeshiGram responsible nahi hoga.

Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy

Food product hone ke karan opened, used ya seal-broken packs normally return nahi kiye ja sakte. Wrong item, damaged pack, leakage ya transit issue hone par delivery ke 24 ghante ke andar clear photo/video aur order details ke saath contact karein.

Eligible case verify hone par replacement, partial refund ya full refund me se suitable resolution diya jayega. Dispatch se pehle cancellation request accept ki ja sakti hai; dispatch ke baad cancellation courier status par depend karegi.

Approved refund original payment method ya mutually agreed mode se process kiya jayega. Bank/UPI processing time DeshiGram ke control se bahar ho sakta hai.
