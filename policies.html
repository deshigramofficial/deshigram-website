Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to process orders and provide support, such as name, phone number, delivery address, order details and payment reference.

Customer information is not sold to advertisers. It may be shared only where necessary for delivery, payment verification, legal compliance or service operations.

Payment Policy

Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI are available at checkout. Available payment options may vary by order or serviceability.

For UPI orders, customers should verify the payment amount and recipient details before payment. A transaction reference or UTR may be requested for confirmation.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Current checkout charges are shown before payment. Logistics / delivery is ₹55, platform handling is ₹10, secure packaging is ₹5 and payment handling is ₹5.

Delivery time depends on destination, courier serviceability, holidays and other operational factors. Customers must provide a correct phone number, PIN code and complete address.

Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy

Opened, used or seal-broken food packs are normally not returnable. For a wrong item, damaged pack, leakage or transit issue, contact DeshiGram within 24 hours with clear photo/video proof and order details.

After verification, an eligible case may be resolved through replacement, partial refund or full refund. Cancellation before dispatch may be accepted; after dispatch it depends on courier status.
