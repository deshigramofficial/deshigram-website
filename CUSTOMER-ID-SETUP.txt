DESHIGRAM CUSTOMER ID + PASSWORD SETUP

Customer-facing login:
- Customer ID = customer's 10-digit mobile number
- Password = customer-created password
- No OTP required
- No email is asked from the customer

IMPORTANT SUPABASE SETTING:
1. Authentication > Providers > Phone: keep Phone provider OFF. No Twilio needed.
2. Authentication > Providers > Email: keep Email provider ON.
3. Turn OFF email confirmation / Confirm email.

The website internally converts the Customer ID to a private synthetic auth identifier. Customers never see or enter an email address.

Review rule remains unchanged: only logged-in customers with an order linked to their account can submit a Verified Customer review.
